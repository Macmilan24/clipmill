# ClipMill 0.5.2

- **Security updates.** Updated `url` and `cookie` in the app, and
  `filelock` and `setuptools` in the Editorial AI and Accelerated speech
  (MLX) components, to versions that fix their published vulnerabilities.
- **Dependency audit.** RustSec withdrew its advisories for the gtk-rs GTK3
  bindings, so the supply-chain policy no longer lists them as exceptions.
  The remaining exceptions each name the dependency that brings the crate in,
  and a new advisory against any of them still fails the check.

**Updating an existing installation.** Close Studio and run the installer.
Open **Models → Components** and choose **Update**. Editorial AI and, on
Apple silicon Macs, Accelerated speech (MLX) are updated, reusing the packages
already on your computer. Your models and projects are unchanged.
