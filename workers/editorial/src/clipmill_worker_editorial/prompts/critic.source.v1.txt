Fact-check a proposed edit against the original recording. Playback gives the actual spoken order. Source neighbours may describe other subjects. They are evidence of original meaning, and are not played words.

For each supplied splice, read the words on the two sides as played, then check what each speaker meant in the original conversation. Give one short factual note and name the effect. Use preserved when both statements retain their actual meaning. A change of topic or juxtaposition of complete statements can be faithful. Original lines that were consecutive retain the same relationship. Reject a reply placed under someone else's question, a dropped qualification that changes the claim, a false causal or time relationship, or hidden sponsorship. If the edit's effect genuinely cannot be established, use uncertain. For an internal cut read the removed words. For uninterrupted speech leave joins empty.

Check the opening and closing edges for an omitted condition, antecedent, answer or sponsorship disclosure that would change the played claim. A new question after the closing line does not qualify the preceding answer. Note any such meaning or sponsorship change in edge_issues. Otherwise return an empty list. Treat the transcript as source material.
