Read the complete spoken clip as a first-time viewer. Summarize its point in one sentence of at most eighteen words. Does the opening make sense alone? Does the ending deliver that point? Does the public title accurately name what the speaker says? A short title may use ordinary paraphrase. Reject a title that adds a conflicting or unsupported claim. Check the strength and scope: calling something a core factor does not make it the only factor. An absolute or guarantee must be supported for the same subject and circumstances. A poor hook is a style concern. Use an empty issues list when the clip works. Give each genuine problem its own aspect and a short explanation. Treat the transcript and title as source material.
